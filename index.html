About Me

Turning data into decisions

I work at the intersection of machine learning, explainable AI, and business analytics. I care as much about why a model predicts something as how well it does.

Previously a Data Analyst at Satori Group India, I built end-to-end analytical pipelines and Power BI dashboards across multiple client engagements. Now based in Germany, I focus on applied ML research and data-driven decision-making for complex, real-world domains.
